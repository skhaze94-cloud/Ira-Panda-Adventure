# v0.9 artwork manifest

Generation mode: **built-in ImageGen**. Final project assets are the twenty
original PNG outputs in `assets/objects`, sampled through `regions.json`.
No generated image was redrawn or composited into another illustration.
The table records each asset's art direction; it is not a verbatim prompt log.
`prompts-recovery.json` retains the available recovery directions.

Shared direction: a rich painterly children's storybook woodland adventure,
tactile natural materials, welcoming goofy expressions, gentle warm light and
moonlit highlights. Objects use an isolated three-quarter view with transparent
margins and no lettering. Bridge images are straight top-down deck panels,
without rails, posts, perspective or a background, for mapping to Godot geometry.

The retained full prompt for the final woods sign was:

> Use case: stylized-concept. A single original Godot game object sprite for the Whispering Woods chapter of a children's storybook woodland adventure. Subject: a carved mossy honey-oak signpost with TWO blank arrow boards pointing left and right, a raised acorn medallion, and a small sleepy adorable owl perched on top. Twisted roots, ivy, soft moss and tactile detailed wood grain. Rich painterly storybook illustration, gentle warm light from upper left with moonlit rim highlights, welcoming to a seven-year-old. Full isolated object including roots, three-quarter isometric view about 30 degrees above, centered with generous transparent margins. Genuinely transparent alpha background. No words, letters, numbers, watermark, frame, scenery or ground plane.
