# Storybook Welcome · v0.11.0

A native Godot interface refresh with preserved painted characters and settings.

- Save-safe Escape cancellation and correct NPC world backdrops.
- Keyboard focus, matching disabled styles, warm gold and botanical ornament.
- Illustrated chapter entries, scrollable short-screen menus and pinned Back controls.
- Music and woodland volume sliders, persistent preferences, larger text choice.
- Pause-to-settings return path, compact story quotations, secondary Skip action.
- Readable score labels, unbroken hearts, lantern cooldown ring and keyboard/touch prompts.
- Gentle title entrance and button feedback governed by Gentler Motion.

Import project.godot in Godot 4.3 or newer; press F5. Validation performed with Godot 4.5.1. Native Linux captures cover 1280×720, 854×480 and 740×360 logical viewports. Physical-device performance remains unmeasured.
